Marketing

Service Overview

Turn online attention into real business growth

About This Service

We help small businesses grow by making them visible online and attracting more customers. Our practical marketing support combines advertising, engaging content and social media management to build awareness and encourage sales.

What We Offer

  • Online Advertising: Promote your business on Facebook, TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp to reach more customers and increase sales.
  • Content Creation: Receive eye-catching posters, flyers, videos and captions designed to grab attention and encourage people to buy.
  • Social Media Management: We post content, reply to customers and help grow your followers.
  • Brand Promotion: Build a strong, consistent brand and become better known in your community.

Customer Benefits

  • Greater online visibility and stronger brand awareness
  • Professional, consistent content that attracts attention
  • More opportunities to connect with potential customers
  • Time saved through managed posting and customer responses
  • Marketing packages designed for different business budgets

Problems We Help Solve

We support businesses that struggle to be seen online, find it difficult to create regular content, lack time to manage social media or need a clearer, more consistent way to communicate their brand.

How to Order

  1. Choose a package: Select the Starter, Growth or Full Business package that suits your goals.
  2. Contact us: Send us your business name, preferred package, products or services, and the platforms you use.
  3. Share your content: Provide your logo, photos, prices and the message you want customers to receive.
  4. Confirm and approve: We confirm the quotation and send prepared content for approval before posting or delivery.

Payment Terms

All prices are quoted in South African Rand (ZAR). The selected package price, payment details and due date are confirmed by quotation before work begins. Work starts once the agreed payment requirement has been received. Please keep proof of payment for your records.

Delivery Process

Approved digital designs and completed content are delivered through the agreed channel, such as WhatsApp or email. Posting is completed on the platforms included in your package. The Growth Package includes 7 days of social media management, while the Full Business Package includes 14 days.
